# 3. Target Market and Audience

The market figures below use the research data (see [Research](02-research.md)). Market sizes are **rough estimates** meant to give a picture, not the result of a formal market study.

## Who uses Vestopia

### Primary audience: beginners who want to learn investing

**Profile**
- Age 17 to 30 (students and young professionals).
- Have heard of stocks, mutual funds or crypto, but have not dared or do not understand yet.
- Comfortable with mobile or PC games, social media and digital payments.
- Do not need to know Web3. An email or Google account is enough.

**Their problems**
- Fear of losing real money.
- Confused by terms like dividend, collateral and liquidation.
- Bored by dull finance courses.

**What Vestopia gives them**
- A place to practice with no risk to real money.
- Financial concepts made visual.
- A first experience with wallets and on-chain transactions, without pressure.

### Second audience: the Web3 and Monad community

**Profile**
- Active wallet users in the Monad or EVM ecosystem.
- Builders, students and communities looking for a lightweight consumer app example.

**What they get**
- An easy-to-try dApp whose activity can be checked in the explorer.
- An entry point to bring non-crypto friends in.

### Third audience: educators and learning communities

**Profile**
- Economics teachers, lecturers, campus investing clubs and financial literacy organizers.
- Need an engaging and safe teaching aid.

**What they get**
- A fun classroom activity with no real money.
- Market event scenarios that can be triggered from the admin panel for class discussion (for example "commodity prices crash, what is the impact?").

## Market size (estimates)

A top-down approach starting from Indonesian data:

| Layer | Estimate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Broad market (TAM)**: Indonesian capital market investors | about 19 million accounts | KSEI, November 2025 |
| **Young segment**: investors under 30 | about 10 million accounts (54% of 19 million) | OJK, September 2025; our rough calculation |
| **Literacy gap**: only 17.78% capital market literacy | most of the young market still needs education | OJK SNLIK 2025 |
| **Initial target (SAM)**: young people comfortable with games and finance apps and open to trying Web3 | tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands | our assumption, untested |
| **Realistic 6-month target (SOM)** | thousands of active players | plan, see [Roadmap](04-roadmap.md) |

Cautions:
- The number of investor accounts is not the number of unique people.
- Only a small share trade actively (estimated at hundreds of thousands per day, secondary source).
- The SAM and SOM figures are early assumptions that need validation through user testing.

Markets outside Indonesia (Southeast Asia, India and global) follow the same low-literacy pattern (about one third of adults are financially literate). The initial focus stays on Indonesia because the team is from there and we have already gathered local data.

## Full segmentation

| Segment | Main need | How to reach | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| University students | Learn stocks with no capital | Campus communities, investing groups, competitions | High |
| Young working beginners | Understand risk before starting | Social media, short video, finance communities | High |
| Crypto beginners | Safe DeFi practice | Monad and EVM communities, Discord, X | Medium |
| Teachers and lecturers | An engaging teaching tool | Teacher training, school collaborations | Medium |

| Teens (under 17) | Meet the idea of money | Needs guidance and an age policy | Low (later) |

## How to reach the market

1. **Hackathon and the Monad community.** Demo Vestopia at Metropolis and in Monad communities to get early players and feedback.
2. **Short content.** 30 to 60 second videos: "Your village got hit by a storm because the debt was too big" is easy to understand and share.
3. **Campuses and communities.** Investing workshops that open with Vestopia as the first activity.
4. **Weekly challenges.** For example "build a village worth 1,000 Coins without getting hit by a storm", with a leaderboard.
5. **Education partnerships.** Work with financial literacy organizers and, where possible, brokerages that want to educate prospective customers.

## Sustainability model (early ideas, not running)

Vestopia on testnet is free and has no monetary value. Possible directions:
- **School or community plans** with a class panel, custom scenarios and progress reports.
- **Education partnerships** with financial institutions that want to reach young prospective investors.
- **Ecosystem grants** from Monad or developer programs.
- **Cosmetic items** for the village (not affecting investing outcomes), if ever used.

We deliberately do not sell real financial products, because the main goal is education and risk must be kept in check.

## Ethics and compliance considerations

- All assets are simulated. This is not investment advice, and the app says so clearly.
- No real money. If a version with real value ever exists, it needs a contract audit and local legal review first.
- The design must not push players toward gambling or excessive risk (see the AMF warning in [Research](02-research.md)).
- Young players need special attention for privacy and age.
